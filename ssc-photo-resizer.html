How to make the SSC photo pass

  1. Take the photo against a plain white or light wall, in even light. No shadow on the face or behind the head.
  2. Keep the face straight, eyes open, and the whole head and hair visible. Some SSC notices ask for a specific head position, so read the notice.
  3. Do not wear glasses if the notification says otherwise, and avoid filters or edited photos.
  4. Upload the photo above, crop it to 276 × 354 pixels, and download the JPG.
  5. Check the file size is between 20 KB and 50 KB before you upload it to the SSC portal.

Common SSC upload errors

  • "Photo size should be 3.5 x 4.5 cm" — you uploaded the right photo but the wrong pixel size. Use 276 × 354 pixels at 200 DPI.
  • "File size should be between 20 KB and 50 KB" — set the minimum and maximum KB and let the tool compress the JPG.
  • "Image format not supported" — save as JPG, not PNG or HEIC.
  • "Photo is not clear" or the face looks dark — retake the photo with more light in front of you, not behind.
  • Signature rejected — use the signature resizer below with cleanup turned on, because a signature shrunk to 140 × 60 pixels turns grey without it.

SSC signature and thumb impression

SSC also asks for a signature and, in some exams, a left thumb impression on white paper. The signature is usually 4 × 2 cm or 6 × 2 cm at 10–20 KB, which is 140 × 60 pixels or 240 × 80 pixels at 200 DPI. Sign in black ink on white paper, photograph it flat, and resize it with the SSC signature resizer.

Sizes and KB limits differ between SSC notifications. Confirm the numbers in the current notice before you submit, and always preview the upload in the portal.

Questions about the SSC photo resizer

What DPI does SSC want?

200 DPI is the usual value in SSC notifications. If your notice mentions a different DPI, change the DPI box in the tool and the pixel size updates automatically.

Should the background be white or light blue?

Follow the notice. Most SSC notices ask for a plain white or light background. If it says white, keep white rather than light blue.

Can I upload the same photo to several exams?

Yes, but the file must match each portal's own rule for that exam. Resize it again for each portal rather than reusing one file everywhere.

Why is my file rejected even when the size is right?

Portals also check the background, the lighting, the date of the photo and whether the file was edited. The size is only one of the checks.
