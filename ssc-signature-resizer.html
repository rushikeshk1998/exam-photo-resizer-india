Why small signatures look blurry

A phone photo of a signature is usually 3000 pixels wide. Shrinking it to 140 pixels wide means every pen stroke is thinner than one pixel, so the JPG encoder averages it into grey. Compressing that grey result to 10 KB removes what little contrast was left.

Turning on signature cleanup fixes it in the right order. The tool converts the image to high contrast, makes the black strokes one pixel thicker, and only then shrinks and compresses the image. The result reads as black ink instead of a grey blur.

How to take a good signature photo

  1. Use a plain white sheet, with no lines or pattern behind the signature.
  2. Sign in black ink. Blue ink is accepted by some exams and rejected by others, so check the notice.
  3. Photograph the paper straight from above in daylight, so the paper fills the frame and there is no shadow.
  4. Leave a little white margin around the writing, then crop tightly in the tool.
  5. Do not sign in capital letters if the notice says otherwise, and sign exactly as you signed in the form.

If your file is under 10 KB

A clean 140 × 60 pixel signature is usually only 3 to 5 KB, so it can be smaller than the minimum the portal asks for. Turn on "reach the minimum KB" and the tool adds very light noise until the file is large enough, without visibly changing the signature.

Check the current SSC notification for the exact signature size and ink colour. Some exams ask for a different width, and some portals check that the signature image is not a photocopy.

Questions about the SSC signature resizer

What size should my SSC signature be?

Most SSC notifications ask for 4 x 2 cm or 6 x 2 cm at 10-20 KB, which is 140 x 60 pixels or 240 x 80 pixels at 200 DPI.

Should I sign in black or blue ink?

Black ink is accepted by most exams. Some notices accept blue ink for the signature and ask for blue or black for the thumb impression. Read the notice for your exam.

Why is my signature grey after compressing?

Thin strokes are averaged into grey when a large photo is shrunk to a very small size. Signature cleanup darkens and thickens the strokes before the resize.

Can I sign with a digital signature?

No. Portals that ask for a signature image expect a handwritten signature on white paper, photographed and uploaded as a JPG.
