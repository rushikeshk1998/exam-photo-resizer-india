Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Short version

Your photo, signature and documents are processed inside your own browser. We never receive them, we never store them, and there is no account to create.

Images and files you open

The resizing, cropping, compression and file conversion happen locally in your browser using the HTML canvas API. The file is read from your device, drawn on a canvas in your browser, and written out as a download. No image data is sent to us, because we have no server that receives it.

The ZIP archive on the Size converter 4-file kit page is also built in your browser.

What we store

We store nothing about the images you open. If you contact us by email, we keep that message and the address it came from for as long as needed to answer you.

Cookies, advertising and analytics

This site may display advertising. Advertising partners, including Google AdSense, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads and measure them. Google may use your device's advertising identifier to serve personalised ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, and you can review third-party cookie options in your browser.

If we add analytics in future, it will be used only to count visits and understand which tools are useful. No information about the images you open is sent to analytics.

Because the ads and any analytics run as third-party scripts on the same page, those scripts can technically read the page while it is open. They do not receive your image files, since your files stay inside the browser canvas and are never attached to any request.

Links to other websites

Our pages link to official exam and government websites for information. We do not control those sites and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

This is a general purpose utility tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the site changes. The date at the top always shows the latest version.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
