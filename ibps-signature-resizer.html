How to keep a 140 × 60 signature clear

  1. Write the signature on plain white paper with a black pen, roughly the size of a passport photo.
  2. Photograph the paper flat from directly above in daylight. A tilted camera makes the ink look thin and grey.
  3. Crop close to the writing so the strokes fill the frame. Empty white space wastes the 140 × 60 pixels you have.
  4. Leave cleanup on, then set ink thickness to 1. If strokes break up, try 2.
  5. Download the JPG and check that the preview in the banking portal still looks like your signature.

The other three files

  • Photograph — 200 × 230 pixels, 20–50 KB. Plain background, face covering most of the frame.
  • Left thumb impression — 240 × 240 pixels, 20–50 KB. Ink on white paper, thumb fully inside the frame.
  • Handwritten declaration — 800 × 400 pixels, 50–100 KB. Write the exact text from your application, then use document mode to whiten the paper.

Questions about the IBPS signature resizer

What size is an IBPS signature?

140 x 60 pixels at 200 DPI, which is 3.5 x 1.5 cm, with a file size between 10 KB and 20 KB.

Does the signature have to be in lower case?

Most notices ask for a signature in lower case or in the same style used on the application form. Sign the same way everywhere so it matches.

My signature file is only 4 KB. Is that a problem?

It can be, if the portal insists on a 10 KB minimum. Turn on the light noise option to lift the file size into the range without changing how the signature looks.

Why was my earlier signature rejected?

The most common reasons are a blurry or low contrast signature, capital letters, or a file outside the 10-20 KB range. Check all three.
