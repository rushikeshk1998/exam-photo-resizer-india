Use the name and date strip when the notice asks

Several notices say the photo must have the candidate's name and the date of photo written or printed at the bottom. Turn on "print name and date on the image", type your name exactly as on the application, pick the date the photo was taken, and the strip is added to the bottom of the resized image.

Photo rules for civil service applications

  • Face should cover most of the frame, with head and hair fully visible.
  • Plain white or light background, even lighting, no shadows on the face or behind you.
  • No sunglasses. Glasses are usually allowed but some notices ask you to remove them.
  • No filters, no beautification, no collage with other photos.
  • Recent photo, taken within the period mentioned in the notice.

If the portal shows a different size

Notices are updated and sizes are changed for particular cycles. If the portal shows a size that does not match the preset, type the pixel values from the portal into the width and height boxes, set the DPI, and the tool will work from that instead.

Questions about the UPSC photo resizer

What size photo does UPSC ask for?

Most civil service applications ask for a square photo, commonly 350 x 350 pixels and above, with a wide file size range of about 20 to 300 KB. Check your specific notice.

Does UPSC need the name and date on the photo?

Many notices ask for the candidate's name and the date of the photo to be printed at the bottom of the image. The name and date strip adds that for you.

Can I wear glasses in my UPSC photo?

Most notices allow plain glasses but ask you to remove sunglasses. Some also ask you to remove glasses if your eyes are clearly visible without them. Follow the notice.

The portal wants a different size than the preset. Can I change it?

Yes. Type the pixel size from the portal into the width and height boxes and set the DPI. The tool works from whatever you enter.
