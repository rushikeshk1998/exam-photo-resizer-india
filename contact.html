Support

Contact

Found a size that is wrong for your exam, or a tool that is not behaving? Tell us. Corrections get added to the presets and the fix helps every candidate who uses it.

Email

Write to help@example.com. Replace the address with your own before going live.

What to include

  • The exam or portal name, for example IBPS Clerk or MPSC Combined.
  • The exact size from the notification, in centimetres or pixels.
  • The DPI and the file size range if the notice mentions them.
  • A screenshot of the notice. Please do not send your own photo or signature.

Reporting a problem with the tool

  • Which browser and phone you are using.
  • Which step failed: upload, crop, resize or download.
  • The error message shown on screen, if any.

Please do not send

Do not email your photo, signature, thumb impression, Aadhaar, PAN card or any other document. We do not need them to fix a preset, and we would rather not receive them.
