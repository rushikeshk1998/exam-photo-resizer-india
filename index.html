How to fix a rejected upload

Most rejections are not about the picture itself. They are about the file. Work through these in order:

  • "Image should be between 20 KB and 50 KB." Upload a JPG, set the minimum and maximum KB, and let the tool compress it. Keep the pixel size exactly as the portal expects.
  • "Signature is not clear" or looks blurry. A 4000-pixel photo shrunk to 140 × 60 turns thin strokes grey. Turn on signature cleanup, crop tightly around the signature, and raise ink thickness to 1 or 2.
  • "Photo size does not match." Check both numbers. 3.5 × 4.5 cm at 200 DPI is 276 × 354 pixels, not 350 × 450.
  • "Image format not supported." Use JPG. Some portals reject PNG even when the pixels are right.
  • "Photo is too old" or the date does not match. Use the name and date strip so the date of photo is printed on the image.
  • Background or shadow problems. Use document mode to whiten the background, or retake the photo against a plain wall in even light.

How centimetres convert to pixels

One inch is 2.54 cm. Multiply the size in centimetres by the DPI and divide by 2.54:

pixels = cm ÷ 2.54 × DPI

So a 3.5 cm wide photo at 200 DPI is 3.5 ÷ 2.54 × 200 = 276 pixels, and 4.5 cm is 354 pixels. At 300 DPI the same photo becomes 413 × 531 pixels. The tool fills in both columns for you, so you never have to calculate it by hand.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded to your server?

No. The image is resized in your browser with the canvas API. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you download the file.

Why does my signature look blurry after compressing it?

Thin pen strokes fade when a large photo is shrunk to a small size like 140 × 60 pixels. Turn on signature cleanup. It darkens the strokes and makes them slightly thicker before the resize, which keeps the signature readable.

What DPI should I choose?

Use the DPI written in the notification. 200 DPI is the usual value for exam photos and signatures. If the portal only gives you centimetres, choose 200 DPI and use the pixel values shown next to it.

Can I use PNG instead of JPG?

You can, but JPG gives you much better control over file size. Most exam portals accept JPG or JPEG only, so JPG is the safer choice.

My file is smaller than the minimum KB. What now?

Small images rarely reach a minimum size. For example, a clean 140 × 60 pixel signature is often only 3 to 5 KB. Turn on "reach the minimum KB" and the tool adds very light noise until the file is large enough without visibly changing the picture.

Does this guarantee that the portal accepts my file?

No. Portals also check the background, the date, the face position, the ink colour and whether the file was taken recently. This tool only handles the size, DPI and file size. Always preview the upload in the portal.
